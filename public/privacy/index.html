Privacy

Shouting Chickens processes microphone and optional camera input on your device so you can play. The MVP has no account, analytics, advertising, backend, or media upload.

Microphone

The game requests microphone access only after you select Enable microphone. It measures sound energy for calibration and controls. It does not perform speech recognition, save audio, or send audio off-device. You can play with keyboard or touch instead.

Optional camera

Camera access is separate and is requested only after you enable it during a run. Video is displayed locally behind the game. It is not recorded, stored, or uploaded. Turning the camera off stops its video track.

Storage and network

This MVP does not persist calibration, scores, settings, or replays. The host serves the application files over HTTPS; the game itself has no gameplay API or telemetry endpoint.

Your controls

Use the game’s fallback controls at any time. You can also revoke microphone or camera permission in your browser or operating-system settings. Reloading or closing the tab stops active media tracks.

Voice calibration should use a comfortable range. Painful shouting is never required.
