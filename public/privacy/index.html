Privacy

Shouting Chickens processes microphone and optional camera input on your device so you can play. The MVP has no account, analytics, advertising, backend, or media upload.

Microphone

The game requests microphone access only after you select Enable microphone. It measures sound energy for calibration and controls. It does not perform speech recognition or send audio off-device. During the comfortable- and strong-voice calibration steps, it can hold the latest brief recording in this tab so you can play it back. That recording is not uploaded, downloaded, or saved to persistent storage. It is replaced or discarded when you advance, retry, leave calibration, choose fallback controls, reload, or close the tab. Gameplay measures sound energy and does not record voice clips. You can play with keyboard or touch instead.

Optional camera

Camera access is separate and is requested only after you enable it during a run. Video is displayed locally behind the game. It is not recorded, stored, or uploaded. Turning the camera off stops its video track.

Storage and network

The game stores one versioned browser record containing derived calibration thresholds, completed-run bests and statistics, camera preference, mute, reduced motion, screen shake, preferred controls, and the safety-copy version. It never stores raw voice samples, calibration playback, camera video, or media recordings. You can remove every Shouting Chickens key from Accessibility & settings without clearing unrelated origin storage. The host serves application files over HTTPS; the game itself has no gameplay API or telemetry endpoint.

Your controls

Use Space, Arrow Up, or playfield press-and-hold at any time. You can recalibrate, adjust the derived threshold within safe bounds, mute the game, reduce motion, disable screen shake, pause, reset local data, or revoke microphone/camera permission. Reloading or closing the tab stops active media tracks. A saved camera preference never starts the camera automatically.

Voice calibration should use a comfortable range. Painful shouting is never required.
